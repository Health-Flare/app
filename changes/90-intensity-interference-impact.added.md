- The symptom form now asks only what it needs: what you're feeling, how
  intense it was, and when. Everything else is offered by name under "Add if
  it helps": where, how much it got in the way, what it stopped you doing,
  and anything else. Tap one to add it. "Why we ask" explains where the
  questions come from.
- "How intense was it?" and "How much did it get in the way?" are recorded
  separately, and "What did it stop you doing, or make harder?" is kept in
  your own words.
- When you log a symptom you've logged before, the details you added last
  time for it open on their own, empty, ready to fill in.
- If you haven't used "Where" or "Anything else" in your last 10 entries,
  they fold away under "2 more", with a one-time note saying so. Turn on
  "Show all symptom options" in the profile to keep everything showing.
- Sleep entries can record "Did you wake up rested?" (No, Partly, Yes).
- Reports carry the new answers: the CSV adds "Got in the way" and "Impact"
  columns at the end, and the PDF adds a "What it stopped me doing" section
  quoting each answer as written.
- Settings has a new "Where our questions come from" page crediting Dr Cat
  Hicks and her Informed Patient method, with every source listed.
