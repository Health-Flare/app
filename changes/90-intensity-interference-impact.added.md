- The symptom form now asks "How intense was it?" and, separately, "How much
  did it get in the way?", plus "What did it stop you doing, or make harder?"
  in your own words. The last field is now "Anything else we didn't ask
  about?". Only the symptom name and intensity are required, as before.
- Sleep entries can record "Did you wake up rested?" (No, Partly, Yes).
- Reports carry the new answers: the CSV adds "Got in the way" and "Impact"
  columns at the end, and the PDF adds a "What it stopped me doing" section
  quoting each answer as written.
- Settings has a new "Where our questions come from" page crediting Dr Cat
  Hicks and her Informed Patient method, with every source listed.
