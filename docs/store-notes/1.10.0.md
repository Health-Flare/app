# Store release notes: 1.10.0

Paste these into each store when submitting 1.10.0. The in-app What's new is separate (`assets/whats_new/releases.json`).

## App Store Connect: "What's New in This Version"

1,192 of 4,000 characters. App Store Connect > the app > iOS App (and macOS App) > 1.10.0 > What's New in This Version.

```
Health Flare is now in four sections: Dashboard, Track, Care and Journal. Nothing has been removed. Meds, Meals and Sleep are tabs inside them now. A short guide on the dashboard shows where everything went, and can put your bottom bar back close to how it was.

You can also make the app fit you. Settings > Your layout chooses what's in the bottom bar and turns off features a profile doesn't use. Turning a feature off never deletes anything, and your layout is kept in backups.

Also in this update:
• Settings > What's new lists every update.
• Insights charts your vitals, including temperature and blood pressure.
• Each profile can show temperatures in °C or °F. Readings are kept exactly as entered.
• Doses and appointments can move to another profile. Health Flare asks first and says what won't carry over.
• Before an update changes how your data is stored, a safety copy is saved on your phone. If an update can't finish, the app still opens on your data and tells you what to do.
• CSV exports say whether each dose was taken, skipped or missed.
• Appointments that have passed no longer show as upcoming.
• Labels and buttons are easier to read, including at large text sizes.
```

## Screenshots

There's one upload set per store and device. It's built from the captures in `screenshots/appstore/` and `screenshots/playstore/`, numbered in upload order:

```bash
bash scripts/store_upload_set.sh        # builds build/store_upload/, then opens it
```

| Store | Slot | Folder to upload | Count (store max) |
|---|---|---|---|
| App Store | iPhone with Dynamic Island, medium display (**required**) | `build/store_upload/app-store/iphone-6.3/` | 10 (10) |
| App Store | iPhone with Dynamic Island, large display | `build/store_upload/app-store/iphone-6.9/` | 10 (10) |
| App Store | iPad 13" display (**required**) | `build/store_upload/app-store/ipad-13/` | 10 (10) |
| Google Play | Phone | `build/store_upload/play-store/phone/` | 8 (8) |

Delete the old screenshots in each slot first, then drag the whole folder in. The file names keep the order.

App Store Connect names the slots by display, not by size. The slot names come from Apple's screenshot specifications, checked October 2026:

- **"iPhone with Dynamic Island (medium display)" is required.** It's captured on iPhone 17 at 1206 × 2622.
- **"iPad 13-inch display" is required** because the app is universal.
- **The large-display slot is optional.** We upload 1320 × 2868 so large phones get sharp shots.
- **Leave the "iPhone with Face ID (large display)" slot empty.** Apple fills it by scaling. If it still holds old 6.5" shots, delete them.
- **iPhone Duo** (outer and inner displays) becomes required from April 2027 for builds on the iOS 27.1 SDK or later. 1.10.0 is built on the iOS 26.5 SDK, so it isn't needed yet; it's a fast-follow.
- **Product page header and search result creative assets** (iOS 27) are optional and aren't part of this release.

## Before uploading

- The captures must be from the release build's code: `bash scripts/take_screenshots.sh` and `bash scripts/take_screenshots_android.sh`, on the tagged commit.
- Look through `build/store_upload/` once. The bottom bar should be Dashboard, Track, Care, Journal, and the chip labels should be readable.
