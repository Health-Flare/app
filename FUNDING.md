# Supporting HealthFlare

HealthFlare is free and open source. If it's useful to you:

- **[Sponsor on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/Health-Flare)**
- **[Support on Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/health-flare/projects/app)**:
  public ledger, this project's costs and income specifically.

See the [org-level funding policy](https://github.com/Health-Flare/.github/blob/main/FUNDING.md)
for what funds go toward and how they're tracked. Costs specific to
HealthFlare (app store account, any backend it depends on) are broken out on
its Open Collective project page.
